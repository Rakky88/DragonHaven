# DragonHaven v0.06.18

- Trial rankings now keep your score in view and separate Basic and Ascended records when Ascended Trials are available.
- Available Trials can be exchanged for another eligible Trial after watching a rewarded ad.
- Keeper identity, level rewards and free-reward cards have clearer layouts, while invalid Keeper names are blocked consistently.
- Nameweaver's Quills have improved chest odds, including a guaranteed additional Quill from Sinister Chests.
- Server-backed actions use clearer blocking feedback where confirmation is required, and stale inventory revisions recover more reliably.
- About DragonHaven once again offers sharing and updates, with automatic Google Play links in Play Store builds.
