# DragonHaven v0.05.12

- Notifies registered keepers at startup when a newer DragonHaven version is available, with a direct update button and a dismissible Later option.
- Fixes the Seven-day Trial Constellation so a day is earned only after completing a Trial and never more than once per calendar day.
- Keeps background music stable while opening Android's notification shade and prevents unintended volume changes during that interaction.
- Improves Android tablet compatibility with resizable portrait and landscape layouts.
