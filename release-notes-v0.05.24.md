DragonHaven v0.05.24

- Special Adventure dragon selection shows all three Expertises and groups fully highlighted dragons, ordered by total Expertise.
- Halloween alternates pumpkin memory and an illustrated tracing path where your finger leaves twinkling sparkles. The timing game has been removed.

- New painted sprites enhance Christmas, New Year, Valentine and Pride Trials.
