Account and privacy improvements:
- DragonHaven now requires an internet connection and a verified account before playing.
- Losing the connection or sending the app to the background ends an active minigame; saved progress is retained.
- New players confirm they are 16 or older and read the privacy notice before registering. Keeper-name entry and the starter egg follow email verification.
- Existing players keep their saved progress and receive the privacy confirmation once.
- Recovery saves are separated by account, with an explicit choice when device and cloud saves differ.
- Crash and performance diagnostics are optional, off by default, and available in Account Info > Privacy.
- Account switching and pending Egg Altar requests have additional recovery protection.
