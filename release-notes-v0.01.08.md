# DragonHaven v0.01.08

This release expands collection and presentation systems while preserving existing saves.

- Adds the 99-gem Portrait Chest and 100 distinct non-collectible-dragon account portraits: 88 Common, 5 Rare, 3 Very Rare, 2 Legendary, 1 Infernal and 1 Mythical.
- Portrait Chest contents are chosen only when opened, always reveal an unowned portrait, appear enlarged after opening and never replace the active portrait automatically.
- Complete portrait collections cannot buy or open another Portrait Chest; the chest and gems remain untouched and a clear warning explains why.
- Adds a 99-coin Title Chest using the same polished opening scene. It reveals one of 500 unique account titles only when opened, never auto-equips the reward and blocks buying/opening safely once the collection is complete.
- Every new or migrated title-less account starts with one random localized title; every new or portrait-less account starts with one random Common portrait. Both starter choices are immediately active.
- Keeps both special chest cards focused by showing collection totals only on Account Info, not in the Shop.
- Rebuilds the Shop as Coins and Gems sections, each with Furniture, Chests and Buy subsections. Real-money packs stay safely disabled pending store products and receipt validation.
- Updates chest odds to the requested Short, Long and Group Adventure distributions.
- Adds twenty-frame reveal animations for each consumable Relic.
- Adds the humorous **Are We There Yet?** achievement for completing 1,000 Adventures and its custom badge.
- Adds **Profile Picture Perfect** and **Highly Titled** for the first successful Portrait Chest and Title Chest opening, each with a custom badge and translations.
- Makes the overflow-menu achievement total dynamic, so it now correctly shows 23 and automatically follows future additions.
- Furniture purchases now go to Inventory unplaced and only trigger placement progress after the player places them.
- Corrects favorite-change achievement logic and migrates away the old automatic unlock.
- Removes duplicate floor summary/currency UI and the redundant Relics introduction card.
- Expands Tower dragon roaming across the usable room instead of clustering around the center.
- Includes complete translations for all nine supported languages and refreshed save migrations.
