# DragonHaven v0.00.09

This hotfix contains the complete v0.00.08 experience and fixes the final
Draconomicon detail issue found during the post-release emulator showcase.

- Hatching plays first, includes mandatory naming, and is followed by
  evolutions from the oldest acquired dragon to the newest.
- Achievement reveals always play last with their new spinning badge sequence.
- Calm tower music loops continuously and resumes cleanly with the app.
- All visible content is translated across all nine supported languages.
- Expanding a discovered dragon family now reliably renders all five collected
  forms without a PageStorage type collision.
- Normal and Spectral family expansion states are stored independently.
- Bottom navigation now follows Adventure, Stash, Tower, Friends and Shop.
- My dragons and Draconomicon are immediately available at the top of Tower;
  hatched dragons no longer appear on the Rooftop Nest floor.
- Earned chests are managed and opened from Stash instead of Adventure.
- All six chest sprites now have true transparency, and the chest-opening
  sequence uses a centered, responsive treasure presentation.
- Android music playback now uses explicit game audio attributes, a reliably
  audible ambient level and non-blocking sound-effect calls.
- The emulator showcase contains every normal and Spectral form, all 20 tower
  floors, all 200 furniture items and every achievement.
