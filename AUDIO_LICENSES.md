# DragonHaven audio sources

All audio shipped with DragonHaven is available under Creative Commons Zero
(CC0 1.0). Attribution is not required by the licence, but the creators and
original source pages are recorded here deliberately.

## Ambient music

| DragonHaven resource | Original work | Creator | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tower_day.ogg` | Sunset Walk | KiluaBoy | https://opengameart.org/node/87082 |
| `tower_night.ogg` | Cathedral in the forest | congusbongus | https://opengameart.org/content/cathedral-in-the-forest-ambient-loop |
| `room.ogg` | Heavenly Loop | isaiah658 | https://opengameart.org/content/heavenly-loop |
| `reveal.mp3` | Once Upon a Time (loop) | TAD | https://opengameart.org/content/once-upon-a-time-loop |

Each source page identifies the work as CC0 and provides the original audio
download. The files are included unmodified and renamed only to match their
runtime scene IDs.

## Interface and game sound effects

The interface click, book, coin, latch, creak, door, metal and short musical
effects come from these Kenney packs:

- UI Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio
- RPG Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
- Music Jingles: https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles

All three official pack pages identify the assets as CC0. Individual files are
included unmodified and renamed to stable DragonHaven event IDs. The bundled
Kenney licence permits personal and commercial use, modification and
redistribution without requiring attribution.
