# DragonHaven audio sources

DragonHaven's original ambient tracks and sound effects are available under
Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0). The optional classical recording is in the
public domain. Attribution is not required, but the creators and original
source pages are recorded here deliberately.

## Classic music

| DragonHaven resource | Original work | Composer | Recording source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `reverie.ogg` | Rêverie, L. 68 | Claude Debussy | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Reverie.ogg | Wikimedia Public Domain Mark; source/performance metadata is incomplete |

The recording is included unmodified from Wikimedia Commons. It is used when
the player selects the **Classic** music style. Wikimedia identifies the file
as free of known copyright and neighbouring-right restrictions, but its page
also lists the recording source as unknown and flags a missing United States
public-domain tag. Before commercial app-store distribution, replace it with a
recording whose performer and recording rights are explicitly documented, or
obtain a release-specific legal review.

## Interface and game sound effects

The interface click, book, coin, latch, creak, door, metal and short musical
effects come from these Kenney packs:

- UI Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio
- RPG Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
- Music Jingles: https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles

All three official pack pages identify the assets as CC0. Individual files are
included unmodified and renamed to stable DragonHaven event IDs. The bundled
Kenney licence permits personal and commercial use, modification and
redistribution without requiring attribution.

`chest_mythical.wav` and `chest_sinister.wav` are original, procedurally
authored DragonHaven effects generated by
`tool/generate_chest_sound_effects.dart`; they contain no third-party samples.
