# DragonHaven v0.05.00

- Friend Messages let friends chat directly. Only the latest 24 hours are
  retained, unread conversations are marked in the Friends list and message
  notifications and incoming messages can be controlled separately.
- Conclaves bring up to twenty Keepers together in a shared Aerie. They include
  twenty emblems, three ranks, public/request/invite membership, a 24-hour
  chat, shareable achievements and a lasting Conclave Chronicle.
- Aeries grow through ten visual stages across fifty Conclave levels. Daily
  contribution limits keep the largest Aerie at least half a year away.
- Conclave names are selected when the Conclave is founded, are unique without
  regard to capitalization or surrounding spaces, and cannot be renamed later.
- Android notifications now target the exact gameplay deadline and pending
  alarms are safely restored after permission changes, reboot, app updates and
  clock or time-zone changes. Expired alarms are not delivered late.
- The Founding Supporter furniture now appears correctly after placement, and
  its portrait uses the same visual scale as other portraits.
- Supabase migrations 30–31 add the private messaging and Conclave server
  model, locked-down RPC access, rate limits, role guards and automatic
  ephemeral-chat cleanup while preserving existing Friends, trades and Group
  Adventures.
