# DragonHaven v0.05.03

- Trial rankings now compare Keeper best scores for Cavern Flight, Ruin
  Breaker and Runeweaver across the world, accepted friends and Conclave
  members. World rankings show the top 100 and always include your own place.
- Dragon chat emotes can now be collected from chests and S+ Trial rewards or
  obtained through three distinct cosmetic packs, then used without limits in
  Friend Messages and Conclave chat.
- Ascension screens now make both the required level and total expertise
  requirement clear before a dragon can evolve.
- The tutorial tracks scrolling and target visibility more reliably, the
  Draconomicon has a richer presentation, and dragons in Tower rooms use stable
  depth ordering.
- Keeper portraits and frames remain circular across Account Info and social
  lists. Badges now sit at the lower left, partly across both portrait and
  selected frame.
- Conclave Keepers have better aligned vanity layouts, can be invited as
  friends when appropriate, and every Conclave chat message shows its send
  time.
- Completed Adventures show a yellow count badge in the main navigation, and
  all random reward sources and odds are documented in the included reference.
- The corrected Supernova Sovereign artwork no longer contains an unintended
  transparent background gap.
