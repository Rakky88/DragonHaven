# DragonHaven v0.06.14

- Dragons can grow to level 50 while their existing evolution path stays unchanged.
- XP earned by a level-50 dragon now advances the Keeper account from level 1 to level 40.
- Every Keeper level awards a Level Chest with a level emote, an egg with a 25% Mythical chance, a level badge and a guaranteed relic. Level 40 also awards a frame and achievement.
- Spirit Alignment now grants five bonus seconds after three consecutive 100% placements. A lower score resets the streak.
- Existing Spirit Alignment rankings start fresh for the updated scoring rule.

Your existing dragons, inventory and progress are preserved.
