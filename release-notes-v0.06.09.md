# DragonHaven v0.06.09

- Adds Spirit Alignment, Ruin Guard and Rune Orbit to Dragon Trials, each with
  its own high-score ranking and focus-specific dragon requirements.
- Balances new Trial offers across Might, Arcana and Spirit, with event Trials
  sharing the rotation during an active event. The new focus games unlock when
  a matching Ascended dragon or a Mastery dragon joins your collection.
- Grants a permanent expertise gift at a dragon's final evolution: +10 to its
  chosen expertise, or +5 to all three expertises for Mastery.
- Makes dismissing a Trial immediate while safely restoring it if the online
  service rejects the change, and restores the restful empty-Trials card.
- Improves Tower room management: clearing a room redistributes dragons or
  gives them a fifteen-minute break, decorating identifies furniture used in
  other rooms, and beds fit cleanly inside the room scene.
