# Rive chest effect attribution

DragonHaven uses the **Particle Burst** Rive animation by
**blackshadow.awesome** as one layer of its chest-opening effects.

- Source: https://rive.app/marketplace/597-1141-particle-burst/
- Runtime file: `assets/animations/chest_particle_burst.riv`
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY)
- Licence text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The animation is recoloured, transformed and combined with original
DragonHaven chest artwork and Flutter-rendered effects at runtime.
