# DragonHaven v0.05.13

DragonHaven's seasonal calendar comes alive with five complete events:
Halloween, Christmas, New Year, Valentine's Day and Pride.

## Seasonal Adventures and Trials

- Discover five event-specific Special Adventures with their own schedules,
  stories, requirements, rewards, chests and eggs.
- Play five original seasonal Trials with full-screen backgrounds, animated
  sprite gameplay, event audio and regular Trial rewards.
- Compete in temporary worldwide event rankings. Podium results remain visible
  for five days and are then preserved in the Seasonal Chronicle.
- Join a true two-Keeper Valentine's Adventure and help the worldwide Pride
  community fill the Haven Spectrum.
- Event Adventures that start before their window closes can still be finished.

## New Special Dragons and treasures

- Raise Gloamgourd, Hollyfrost, Dawnchime, Rosevow and Spectrumplume through all
  six forms, including their Mastery forms and Spectral variants.
- Collect event-specific Special Chests, Special Eggs, achievements, podium
  emotes and temporary public-domain event music.
- Every event keeps its own reward recipe and provenance, so saved chests and
  eggs continue to reveal the correct contents after the event ends.

## Presentation and reliability

- Seasonal screens now carry their event identity through the HUD, phase path,
  ambient motion, Adventure cards, results, rankings and empty states.
- Cutout sprites have clean transparency and safe margins, including compact
  320 x 640 layouts.

This release also adds guarded, server-authoritative seasonal rankings and
reward claims while keeping the existing economy compatibility mode disabled by
default.
