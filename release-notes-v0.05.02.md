# DragonHaven v0.05.02

- Conclave chat now scrolls in the natural direction, follows new messages
  only when you are already near the bottom and offers a quick jump to the
  newest message when you are reading older messages.
- Friend-message notifications are checked more frequently, refresh
  immediately when the app resumes and retry safely when Android temporarily
  cannot display them.
- Special Adventures show how long they remain available and present their
  rewards more clearly without revealing the contents of a Special Chest.
- Golden Hour now doubles the chance that an eligible hatching dragon becomes
  Spectral, from 5% to 10%.
- Portrait frames preserve the normal portrait size, vanity frames and badges
  stay disabled when deselected, and badges no longer use the unwanted white
  circular holder.
- The Frostfable Wyrmling artwork no longer has a transparent gap in its chest.
- Dragon Academy Dropout achievements reliably receive their full-screen
  reveal, including a one-time recovery for affected existing saves.
- Production support operations gain a service-role-only, privacy-minimized
  lookup with a 30-day hashed access log and automatic cleanup. No player
  inventory, save body, e-mail address or message content is exposed.
