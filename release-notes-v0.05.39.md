DragonHaven v0.05.39

- New Year now keeps accelerating until three mistakes, with no game timer or score cap. S+ starts at 20,000 points.
- Play New Year anywhere in its four screen-wide lanes, tap anywhere to drop a Birthday layer, and swipe anywhere to guide the Valentine hearts.
- Dragons now share one expertise budget: 950 normally, 1,100 for Sinister, with 50 more after Mastery. The MAX badge marks a fully trained dragon.
- Discover a dragon's hidden Dragon Spark with the rare Spark Astrolabe, available from chest and S+ Trial relic drops.
- Half of Mini, Short and Long adventures now shift expertise between skills. Every eligible dragon can join; losses stop at zero before gains are awarded.
- Existing earned expertise and rewards from adventures already underway are preserved.
