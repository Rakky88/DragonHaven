# DragonHaven v0.06.11

- Spirit Alignment now lasts one minute: keep aligning shapes, and earn five extra seconds for every perfect placement. Previously started games retain their original rules.
- Ruin Guard has a new boulder sprite, a dragon lunge and a shattering impact. Rune Orbit moves more smoothly and clearly shows successful and missed taps.
- Improved rewarded-ad presentation, reward recovery and immediate reward feedback after watching an ad.
- Trial rankings now have clearer labels, and the Adventure event banner stays above the tabs.
- Improved small-screen layouts, reduced-motion feedback and translations.

Install **DragonHaven.apk** to update your existing app while keeping your account and saved progress.
