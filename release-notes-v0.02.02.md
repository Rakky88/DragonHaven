# DragonHaven v0.02.02

- Repaired the online Friends refresh that could fail on Android after v0.02.01.
- Limited every account to one active trade at a time, including incoming trades.
- Limited successful trades to three per account per Amsterdam calendar day.
- Trade proposals now expire after ten minutes. After the recipient offers an item, the initiator gets a fresh ten-minute window for the final confirmation.
- Reserved items are released automatically when a trade expires.
- New accounts must verify their unique email address before signing in.
- Fixed verification links so they confirm the account and then open the permanent DragonHaven release page instead of a local error page.
