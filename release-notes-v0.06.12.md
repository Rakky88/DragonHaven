# DragonHaven v0.06.12

- Rewarded ads now dismiss the preparation screen before opening full screen, for both Free gems and Free coins.
- Trial Rankings shows all six standard Trials in two rows: Basic and Ascended.
- Spirit Alignment awards 100% when the smaller shape fits completely inside the outline. Rune Orbit keeps accelerating after every point.
- The Egg Altar offers Moral Prism, Order Compass and Soul Mirror, while preserving existing relic stock.
- Existing relic drop chances are five times higher, capped at 100%. Chests can also drop a separate, tradeable Nameweaver's Quill: 1% Wooden, 2% Silver, 4% Gold, 8% Dragon and 16% Mythical.
- A bound Nameweaver's Quill is available at the top of the relic shop for 100 gems.
- Improved recovery after rejected ad rewards and preserved compatibility for Trials already in progress.

Update to this version to keep playing with the new server data format. Your saved progress is preserved.
