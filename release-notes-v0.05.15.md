# DragonHaven v0.05.15

- Friends and Conclave now have their own tabs at the top of the Friends page.
- A small red Conclave badge counts unread messages from the past 24 hours. Read status is remembered on your device.
- Witchlight Ward Arcana uses similar pumpkin lantern faces to challenge your memory.
- Witchlight Ward Spirit lets you trace a winding path with your finger. Stay inside the edges to reach the lantern.
- Trial results show a cleaner rank emblem without the translucent background stars.
- Active Adventures, including group journeys, are sorted by remaining time, shortest first. Groups waiting to start appear last.

Existing progress and rewards are preserved.
