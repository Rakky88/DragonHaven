# DragonHaven v0.02.03

- Added the secret Mastery evolution for all 42 dragon families, including spectral variants and a secret Mastery achievement.
- Dragon expertise now has a maximum of 300 per stat, with a new achievement for reaching 300 Might, Arcana and Spirit on one dragon.
- Rebalanced Mini, Short, Long and Group adventure durations and rewards. The relevant expertise now shortens every adventure within its stated minimum.
- Group adventures now use the shared server offer and the average relevant expertise of all participating dragons.
- Improved Friends, friend requests, profile details, trade indicators and the combined offline/online account page. Online profiles always mirror the offline keeper name, portrait and title.
- Added a notification when a friend request arrives.
- Enforced one active trade per account, at most three completed trades per Amsterdam day, and one hard ten-minute expiry window from creation.
- Fixed trade inventory synchronisation and expanded supported chest tiers.
- Strengthened new-account password requirements and retained mandatory unique-email verification.
- Refined Group Adventure presentation, the My Dragons banner, and several shared visual components.
