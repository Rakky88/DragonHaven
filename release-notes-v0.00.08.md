# DragonHaven v0.00.08

This release makes DragonHaven’s biggest moments feel like one coherent story.

- Hatching now always plays first and includes the complete, mandatory naming
  moment before anything else can continue.
- Multiple evolutions play one by one, with the oldest acquired dragon first.
- Achievement reveals wait until the end, then spin their full-colour badge
  onto the screen and spin away after a tap.
- The reveal queue is saved, so Android closing the app cannot silently lose a
  hatch, evolution or achievement presentation.
- Calm tower, night, room and reveal music now loops continuously and can
  recover without restarting unnecessarily when the app resumes.
- All visible game content is available in German, English, Spanish, French,
  Italian, Dutch, Portuguese, Simplified Chinese and Japanese—including 800
  Adventures, 200 furniture items, 300 dragon sayings, achievements, egg clues,
  notifications and room interactions.
- A safe non-persistent showcase build contains every normal and Spectral
  dragon form, every floor and item, the full Draconomicon and every
  achievement in the emulator.
