# DragonHaven v0.06.05

- Restores the v0.05.40 Rooftop Nest egg picker with its tile/list views,
  sorting controls, hatch times, dates and egg hints.
- Lets you tag or untag Eggs directly in the Inventory grid and list, while
  retaining the historical Egg details sheet and server-backed actions.
- Restores the historical selected-dragon action card, hides Rename without a
  Nameweaver Quill and makes the sanctuary action clear that the dragon leaves
  the Tower.
- Restores Adventure refresh countdowns and pull-to-refresh. Offers refresh at
  their real boundary instead of repeatedly polling in the foreground.
- Tightens and aligns the Dragon Tower header, removes the empty space above
  My Dragons and preserves the correct aspect ratio of dragon artwork.
- Keeps progress server-owned. Immediate safe previews still reconcile with
  the server and roll back if the server refuses them.
